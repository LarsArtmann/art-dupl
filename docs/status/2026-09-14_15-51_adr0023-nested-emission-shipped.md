## Executive Summary

The report's critical path is **DONE**. The composite-statement false negative — "any divergence inside a for/if/switch body masks the ENTIRE composite statement" — is fixed by default: `serial()` now emits nested-block statements as individual tokens after the composite, GenDecl specs stay composite-only, and subsumed units are trimmed at `FindSyntaxUnits` so fully-identical matches keep their pre-change group shape, size, and hash.
