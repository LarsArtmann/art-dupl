01Pareto Breakdown

The 1% that delivers 51% — T1 Type-2 classification fix

The tool's headline feature silently misclassifies results. Every renamed-clone (Type 2, the most common kind in real codebases) is labeled Type 1 because statement serialization drops descendants where the renamed identifiers live. Fix: classifyCloneType walks node.Children directly instead of going through Serialize. Existential correctness, small effort, low risk.

The 4% that delivers 64% — Quick wins + non-destructive serial cluster

Seven XS/S tasks (AnalysisTime tag, GC removal, deterministic sort, MaxChildrenSerial wiring, sha1→sha256, RunTableTest fix, dead-field removal) plus the T5+T6 cluster: non-destructive serial() (Option B side table) paired with cache-miss deep-clone. This cluster fixes the two remaining existential bugs AND unblocks deterministic/golden-file testing. ~3 hrs total.

The 20% that delivers 80% — Robustness + policy compliance

Detection-mode enum unification (kills the --semantic=false silent-drop class), FuncLit normalization (Type-2 inside closures), dead-code purge, and the two policy bans (json v1→v2, sha1). After this tier the engine is correct, deterministic, and policy-compliant.

The other 80% — Architecture, quality, infrastructure, roadmap

DTO decoupling, file splits, comparator unification, cache eviction, branded types, fang v2, watch mode, new languages. Valuable but not urgent — backlog after the 80% is delivered.
